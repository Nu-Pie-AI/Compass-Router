# Jev Pricing Verification

The official TypeSafe website currently publishes Jev at `$0.042` per million input tokens and free output tokens. This is a provider price, not an approved Compass customer price. Compass must still record an approved pricing policy, currency conversion, margin, limits, and commercial terms before managed Jev traffic is enabled.

The code therefore does not auto-enable Jev or infer a customer price from the public page. The seeded model has no verified Compass pricing policy and stays unavailable. BYOK remains zero-charge once the provider, model, credential, and access gates are explicitly approved.

Sources reviewed: official TypeSafe documentation and the official TypeSafe product site. Pricing should be rechecked before production activation because provider pricing and commercial terms can change.
